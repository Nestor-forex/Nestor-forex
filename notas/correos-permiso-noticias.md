2. Baja hasta el título **«Request permission to use Bank of England material»**.
3. Toca el enlace azul de esa frase (dice *request permission to use Bank of

4. Rellena tu nombre y tu correo, y en el cuadro grande de texto pega el correo

**Asunto:** Permission request: headlines and links from your Forex news RSS in a subscription app

I run two small mobile web apps for Forex traders (Nestor Forex Swing and Nestor Forex Intraday). They show market information — currency strength, an economic calendar, interest rates — and they are offered as a paid subscription.

I would like to show, inside the apps, the **headline, publication time and a link** to articles from your Forex news RSS feed (https://www.investing.com/rss/news_1.rss), with "Investing.com" clearly credited as the source. We would not copy the article text, change the headlines, use your logo, or store anything beyond the headline, time and link. Readers would open the full article on investing.com.

We would check the feed at most every four hours.

Your Terms and Conditions (sections 10 and 14) limit use to personal, non-commercial purposes and exclude automated access, so I am asking for your written permission before doing anything. If this is possible, could you tell me the conditions (attribution text, frequency, any fee)?

Thank you,
Néstor

*En español:* pide permiso escrito para mostrar en las apps de pago el titular,
la hora y el enlace de sus noticias de Forex, citando a Investing.com, sin

`FUENTES_PUBLICABLES` con la atribución que pida. **Sin esa respuesta no se
añade nada**, aunque su canal responda.
